# LastingMind 2.0 — Product Overview

## What Is LastingMind?

LastingMind is an AI-powered legacy app where people record their life stories, memories, wisdom, and personal messages. Everything a person shares trains an AI that learns to sound like them — capturing their voice, their values, and their stories. Their loved ones can then interact with this AI, asking questions and connecting with the person's legacy long after they're gone.

The core insight behind LastingMind is simple: the stories that make a person who they are — the ones told at dinner tables, on car rides, at family gatherings — are at risk of being lost. LastingMind exists to preserve them.

---

## Who Is It For?

**Legacy Creators** are the primary users. Typically aged 50–70+, often approaching or in retirement, they are motivated by a desire to leave something meaningful behind for their children and grandchildren. They want to be known — not just remembered — and they want the people they love to have access to their stories, wisdom, and voice long into the future.

**Audience Members** are the secondary users — children, grandchildren, spouses, and close friends of the Legacy Creator. While their engagement is lower while the creator is alive, it becomes deeply meaningful after the creator passes. They interact with the AI, browse stories, and send questions back to the creator.

## Business Context

LastingMind is being developed by Scope Labs for founder Ty Allen. The product is entering its second major version (2.0), with a primary near-term goal of producing a polished, investor-ready prototype that demonstrates the full end-to-end experience. The prototype is a coded, mobile-focused React/HTML build designed to convince investors and enable early user testing.

The broader opportunity sits at the intersection of AI, legacy preservation, and family connection — a growing space as aging demographics increasingly seek meaningful ways to pass down their stories and values.

**Prototype Scope.** The prototype is driven entirely by mock data. There is no live backend, no real AI processing, and no actual user data at this stage. All content — stories, family members, tree states, success screen artifacts, chat responses — will be pre-populated examples designed to simulate a realistic experience. The goal is to make the prototype feel real enough to convey the product's value clearly, not to function as a working product.

## What We're Building

LastingMind 2.0 is a structured, guided experience that takes a Legacy Creator through three phases:

**Phase 1 — Build Your Foundation.** The creator captures the essential facts of their life: family, friends, career, education, values, and favorites. This gives the AI the base knowledge it needs to begin representing them accurately.

**Phase 2 — Tell Your Story.** The creator moves from facts to narrative, walking through the chapters of their life in depth — sharing stories, emotions, and memories that bring their history to life. This is the emotional core of the product.

**Phase 3 — Leave Your Legacy.** The most personal content: letters to loved ones, voice messages, reflections on how they hope to be remembered, and wisdom they want to pass on.

As the creator progresses, a growing tree on the home screen reflects the depth and breadth of what they've shared — serving as both a progress indicator and an emotional symbol of what they're building.

## The App Experience

LastingMind works as a progressive web app on both mobile and desktop. The home screen is organised around three elements: a prompt card that surfaces the user's next suggested story, a visual tree that grows as they contribute, and a scrollable content log (the "tap root") showing everything they've captured.

Sessions are conversational and voice-forward. An AI guide leads the creator through prompts designed to trigger genuine memory and storytelling — using specific, sensory, emotionally warm questions rather than abstract overviews. The approach draws on clinical reminiscence therapy techniques and research in autobiographical memory.

When the creator has shared enough, family members are invited in. They can chat with the creator's AI, browse stories, send questions, and react to entries — creating a living connection between generations that exists while the creator is alive and endures after they're gone.

## The Role of Animation

Animation is not decorative in LastingMind — it is a core part of how the product communicates progress, reward, and emotional resonance. For a user who has just spent ten minutes opening up about a meaningful chapter of their life, the moment after they finish needs to feel like something. Animation is how we deliver that.

**The Tree as a Living Progress Indicator.** The tree on the home screen grows organically as the creator contributes content. After each completed session, the tree visibly responds — a branch extends, new leaves appear, the canopy fills in. This growth animation is the primary reward signal in the app. It should feel alive and natural: not a progress bar incrementing, but a living thing responding to care. Subtle idle animations — a gentle sway of leaves, soft light drifting through the canopy — keep the tree feeling present even between sessions.

**Success Screens.** Every completed module ends with a success screen, and these moments should feel like opening a gift rather than checking off a task. Animations here serve two purposes: they celebrate what the creator just did, and they reveal the artifact they created — a family relationship map, a career timeline, a biography paragraph, a wisdom card. The reveal itself should be animated: content appearing with intention, not snapping into place.

**Phase Milestone Celebrations.** When a creator completes a full phase — finishing their Foundation, completing their Life Story, or delivering their Legacy — the celebration should be the most emotionally impactful moment in the app. These are major animations: the tree growing through a stage transition, new branches and features appearing, a screen that holds the weight of what the person just accomplished. Sound, motion, and copy all work together at these moments.

**Micro-Interactions.** Smaller animations throughout the app reinforce a sense of quality and responsiveness — prompt cards sliding smoothly as the user swipes, nodes in the tap root expanding with a natural ease, the recording interface responding visually to the user's voice. These are not flashy; they are quiet signals that the app is alive and paying attention.

The overall animation language should feel organic and warm — rooted in nature, not technology. Nothing should feel game-like or clinical. Every motion should serve the feeling that something real and meaningful is being built.
